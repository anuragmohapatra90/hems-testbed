02 / Research

Experimental capabilities

Investigate home energy management with real devices and reproducible conditions.

01 / EVALUATE

Evaluation

Investigate HEMS strategies under comparable conditions.

  • Algorithms, forecasts and control windows
  • PV, battery and charging interoperability
  • Grid-friendly behaviour and dynamic tariffs
02 / CURRENT CAPABILITIES

Equipment

Bring real power, real measurements and real devices together in the lab.

  • Real PV inverter with controllable DC source, battery and EV charger
  • Household, SG-Ready and vehicle profiles
  • Measurement at 1-second intervals and data analysis
  • Digital-twin extension for scalable test campaigns
03 / LOOKING AHEAD

ExtensionsPlanned

Extend the test environment with thermal hardware and intelligent metering.

  • Real heat pump and thermal home prosumer
  • iMSys functions: §14a, §9 EEG and full TAF access
  • iMSys extension in cooperation with Fraunhofer IEE

03 / Inside the lab

The HEMS laboratory wall

The setup combines a real PV inverter supplied by a controllable DC source, real storage hardware and a real charging point. Three additional inverters reproduce household demand, a heat pump and an electric car, using real electrical power.

Repeatable experimental conditions

Daily patterns and seasonal conditions can be prescribed and repeated, making it possible to investigate device interoperability alongside the HEMS decisions.

04 / Technical details

Interfaces & operation

A Python-based simulation layer connects reference profiles with hardware and digital models. The PhyLFlex project investigates how different HEMS strategies can be evaluated systematically.

Operating mode
Full prosumer mode: HEMS access to every device, grid import and export
Emulation
Three inverters for household load, SG-Ready heat pump and electric car
Communication
Modbus TCP · MQTT
Measurement & analysis
1-second measurements; data analysis with an open-source repository
Digital integration
Python simulation layer; integration with pandapower / Gridlock
Test conditions
Reference demand and generation profiles, daily patterns and seasons
